## Some of our projects

We believe that both large- and small-scale change is essential if we're going to transform how state government serves Californians. Our work reflects this.

::: {.highlights .light .unnumbered}
::: {.highlight}
### Designing for people
With Alpha.ca.gov, we showed how user research, content design, and technology can make digital services easier to use.
:::
::: {.highlight}
### Correcting search
When the info that comes up in a Google search is wrong, it's a problem. So we're showing departments how to make sure their info is right.
:::
::: {.highlight}
### Equity in data
We're setting a new standard for how government makes data available, ensuring it's accessible and fast, a critical step toward increasing equity.
:::
::: {.highlight}
### Anticipating needs
We've developed a new tool that anticipates and meets user needs by reviewing site searches, updating quick answer topics and content.
:::
::: {.highlight}
### Pioneering accessibility
We're using high- and low-tech methods to establish a new standard of accessibility.
:::
::: {.highlight}
### People research
We're asking Californians directly about their needs, challenges and expectations, helping create a culture of data-driven decision making.
:::
:::
