## What we do

We focus on creating and delivering lasting change, not just quick fixes. Our approach starts by understanding what Californians need and how those needs are being met. We work with state agencies and departments to reimagine the processes, policies, and technology systems that problems are rooted in.

::: {.arrow-list}
- Create solutions
- Raise empathy
- Drive efficiency
- Improve accessibility
- Improve equity
- Frame priorities
- Prototype services
- Build capacity
- Safeguard rights
:::
